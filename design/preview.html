01 · NAME

Sense of the cut

Sense is both perception and direction. A good cutter has a sense of the cut: they read the hide, dodge the scar and know where each piece fits. CutSense puts that sense on screen, and makes it checkable.

02 · PERSONALITY

The workshop master

Patient, precise, honest. Speaks plainly, to you, with the words of the trade: offcut, pattern, waste, notch. When it does not know something, it says so. It never sells an "optimum" it cannot prove.

03 · VISUAL WORLD

Pattern paper and cutting mat

By day, gridded kraft paper; by night, the green self-healing mat. Blue chalk for what the AI proposes, vermilion waxed thread for what your hand does, a green seal for what is verified.

Provenance language

Each origin has its own line, word and colour. Take the colour away and the line and the word still tell who did what.

AI proposalChalk: dashed 8/5. Provisional until you accept it.
Corrected by youThread: solid with vertices. Your hand rules.
ComputedGraphite: thin line, deterministic, same seed = same plan.
VerifiedSeal: thick line + toothed seal. Only the verifier grants it.
Out of dateAmber dots: the data changed after optimizing.
FailedBroken line with a cross: something could not be completed.

VOICE

How CutSense speaks

Plain, warm, craft-literate, honest. Talks to you directly. Short sentences. First what happened, then what to do.

AI proposal

"AI proposes this outline. Check it: you have the last word."

Credit the source; hand the decision to the person.

AI failed

"AI could not read the edge this time. Your photo is still here."

Say what failed, then what was not lost.

Not calibrated

"Not calibrated: relative measurements (u), not centimetres."

Never let a number look more precise than it is.

Verified

"Plan verified. It is the best layout found, not a proven optimum."

Claim what was checked, and nothing more.

Out of date

"You changed something after optimizing. Re-optimize to verify it again."

Name the cause and the one action that fixes it.

Never

"Perfect optimization! You saved 32% of your material 🚀"

Never: hype, unproven savings claims, emoji confetti.
